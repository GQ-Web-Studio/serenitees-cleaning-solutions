Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Serenitees Cleaning Solutions handles personal information when you use this website, contact us, or request a quote.

1. Accountability and scope

Serenitees Cleaning Solutions is responsible for personal information under its control. This policy applies to information collected through this website, the Contact form, the Request a Quote form, email, and telephone communications connected to an inquiry or cleaning service.

Serenitees has designated a Privacy Officer to answer questions, handle access or correction requests, and address privacy concerns. Contact information appears at the end of this policy.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information reasonably needed to respond to you, prepare a quote, arrange a service, or operate and protect the website. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and preferred contact method.
  • Your city or service area, preferred cleaning date, requested service, and cleaning frequency.
  • Details about a residential or commercial property, including bedrooms, bathrooms, property type, areas to be cleaned, and other information you choose to provide.
  • Photos you voluntarily upload to help us understand the space and prepare or discuss a quote.
  • The contents of messages, emails, telephone calls, and other communications with Serenitees.
  • Basic technical and security information that may be recorded automatically by the website host, such as an IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, requested page, and date and time of access.

Please do not submit information or photos about another person unless you have their permission to share them.

3. How we use information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to questions, messages, and service inquiries.
  • Understand cleaning needs, prepare a quote, and discuss availability.
  • Arrange, provide, manage, and follow up on cleaning services.
  • Communicate through the contact method you select.
  • Maintain business records, prevent misuse, troubleshoot issues, and protect the website and its users.
  • Meet legal, accounting, insurance, and regulatory requirements.

We will not use personal information for an unrelated purpose without explaining that purpose and obtaining any consent required by law.

4. Consent and your choices

We seek meaningful consent at or before the time personal information is collected. The forms identify why the information is needed and require you to acknowledge this policy before submitting. Optional fields and photo uploads may be left blank.

You may withdraw consent to a use that depends on consent by contacting the Privacy Officer, subject to reasonable notice and any legal or contractual limits. Withdrawing consent may prevent us from responding to an inquiry, preparing a quote, or providing a requested service.

Serenitees does not use information submitted through these forms to send promotional email or text messages unless you separately agree to receive them.

5. When information is shared

Serenitees does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with service providers that support website hosting, form processing, email, information technology, security, or business administration. These providers receive only the information needed to perform their services and are expected to protect it.

We may also disclose information when required or permitted by law, to respond to lawful requests, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a business transaction where appropriate privacy protections are used.

Some service providers may store or process information outside Ontario or Canada. In that case, the information may be subject to the laws of the place where it is processed.

6. Storage, retention, and safeguards

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy and for applicable legal, accounting, insurance, or business requirements. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to securely delete it, destroy it, or remove identifying details.

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. These may include limiting access, protecting accounts and devices, using reputable service providers, and reviewing how information is handled. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Cookies and third-party services

As of the date above, this website does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, tracking pixels, or browser storage for marketing or behavioural profiling. A cookie consent banner is therefore not displayed.

The site uses Google Fonts to display its typography. When a page loads, your browser may connect to Google and provide technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. You can review the Google Privacy Policy.

The Request a Quote form is currently processed through FormSubmit, which receives the information submitted through that form and forwards it to Serenitees. You can review FormSubmit’s privacy terms. If analytics, advertising tools, or other non-essential tracking technologies are added later, this policy and any required consent choices will be updated before those tools are used.

8. Your privacy rights

You may ask whether Serenitees holds personal information about you, request access to that information, ask how it has been used or disclosed, and request correction of information that is inaccurate or incomplete. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

We will respond within the period required by applicable law. Access may be limited in circumstances permitted by law, and we will explain the reason where required. If a privacy concern is not resolved to your satisfaction, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Children’s privacy

This website and Serenitees cleaning services are intended for adults arranging services for a home or business. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices, service providers, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised last-updated date. Material changes will be highlighted or otherwise communicated when appropriate.
