Who We Are

Serenitees Cleaning Solutions provides dependable residential cleaning and commercial cleaning throughout Durham Region, Toronto GTA, and surrounding areas. We work with homeowners, families, short-term rental hosts, professionals, and businesses to help keep their spaces clean, comfortable, and well maintained.

From one-time cleaning to recurring service, each job is approached with professionalism, attention to detail, and genuine care. Our goal is to make maintaining your space easier while delivering a service you can feel confident relying on.

How Serenitees began

Our Story

Serenitees Cleaning Solutions began with an understanding of how overwhelming it can be to balance work, family, and everyday responsibilities while still trying to keep a home or business looking its best.

The business was created to offer more than just cleaning. From the beginning, the focus has been on providing dependable service built around trust, professionalism, consistency, genuine care, attention to detail, and respect for every client’s space.

The name Serenitees reflects the feeling we want every client to experience after a cleaning: a greater sense of serenity, comfort, and peace of mind.

As Serenitees continues to grow, the goal remains the same: make life easier for the people and businesses we serve, build lasting relationships, and leave every space fresh, healthy, and well maintained.
